# Privacy Statement

**Game:** Mage Rage
**Developer:** Conan Quilty
**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

## Summary

This Game does not collect, store or share any personal data of its own. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers run by the developer that receive information about you or your device.

## Data the game does not collect

- Your name, email address or other contact details
- Your location
- Photos, contacts, microphone or camera data
- Any information sent to the developer or to third parties by the game's own code

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings and game progress locally on your device, using Unity's PlayerPrefs. This data stays on your device and is never sent to the developer. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

| Key | Type | What it stores | Saved when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `haptics` | int (0/1) | Vibration on or off | You change the setting |
| `textScale` | float | Text size multiplier | You change the setting |
| `screenShake` | int (0/1) | Screen shake on or off | You change the setting |
| `wallet.coins` | int | Coins you have earned | Your coins change, and when the game goes to the background |
| `wallet.level` | int | Arcane Power upgrade level | You buy an upgrade, and when the game goes to the background |

None of these values identify you. They are game settings and progress only.

## Data collected by Unity and your platform

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may collect standard technical information as part of normal operation, such as device type, operating system version and crash or performance data. We do not control this collection and do not receive it from the game.

For details, see:

- [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this statement

If this statement changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
